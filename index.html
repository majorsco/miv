Before you ask

Is MIV live yet?

Not yet. We're in early access — this is about joining the waitlist and shaping what launches, not moving money today.

Is this regulated?

We're working through the right registrations before opening real investment slots. We'd rather launch slow and correctly than fast and in trouble — that protects you as much as it protects us.

What counts as a "product"?

Still being finalized. The model stays the same regardless: real, visible activity you can back, with scheduled payouts.

How is this different from the schemes that collapsed before?

Fair question to ask of anyone promising returns. Our answer is visibility and registration, not just a bigger promise — you'll be able to see what you're backing, not just a number going up.

Get in before the doors open

Leave your email and we'll reach out when early access slots are ready — no commitment, no payment, just first in line.
